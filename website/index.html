How it works

Discovery by email or call

You describe the process. We ask about users, data, deadlines and the systems you already pay for.

Written proposal

Scope, stack, timeline, price and what is excluded. Under 1,500 words. You sign off before any code is written.

Build with verification at each stage

Spec, data, backend, screens, deploy. Each step is checked before the next. You see working software early, not at the end.

Handover you own

Accounts in your name, a handover document, and instructions for the changes you will need to make yourself.

Why a specialist

We know the workflow before the first call.

Art approval, decoration methods, vendor MOQs, kitting, drop-ship to recipients, seasonal redemption spikes. Merchtech comes out of a promotional merchandise company that built its own internal tooling first.

Who we build for

Micro, small and medium businesses.

Teams without an IT department and software that must pay back within a season. We default to tools you already have, like Google Workspace and Shopify, and only add servers when the brief requires them.
