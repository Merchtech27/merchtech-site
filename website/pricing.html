Pricing

Every job is quoted in writing before it starts.

Prices depend on scope, so we do not publish a rate card. What we publish is how the engagement works, so there are no surprises.

Three ways to engage

Fixed-price project

A defined deliverable, a written scope with exclusions, a fixed price and a payment schedule. Best for redemption sites and single internal apps.

Scoped quote

For work where discovery is needed before a price is possible. A short paid discovery produces the spec and the fixed quote for the build.

Retainer

A monthly fee for hosting, support and a set number of change hours. For clients whose tools need ongoing care after launch.
