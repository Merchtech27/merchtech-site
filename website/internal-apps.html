Internal apps

Replace the spreadsheet everyone edits at once.

One process, one app. Built on Google Apps Script and Sheets when your team is on Google Workspace, or Node and PostgreSQL when roles, permissions and record counts demand it.

Four apps we build most

Order tracking and production board

Every order line with its status: art approved, in production, shipped. Filters by client, vendor and due date. Role-gated status changes. Email to sales when an order ships.

Client intake portal

A no-login link per client to collect logos, vector artwork, PMS colours, shipping addresses and sign-off. Your team reviews, approves or requests changes. No more chasing by email.

Vendor, SKU and product master

One place for supplier, sourcing cost, MOQ, decoration options and images. Feeds your quotes and your store so the same product is not described five different ways.

Quoting and proposal tools

Price calculators with quantity breaks and decoration charges. Quote documents generated from the product master. Mockup request tracking.

How a build runs

Spec from your spreadsheet

Your current sheet is the specification. Every column is kept or explicitly dropped. Status flow and who can change what are written down and approved before code.

Data and backend

One tab or table per entity, generated ids, timestamps. Writes go through one path so two people cannot overwrite each other.

Screens

A filtered table, a detail view with status actions, a creation form. Plain HTML and JavaScript, no framework to maintain.

Notifications and access

Email on the events you name. Access restricted to your domain, or tokenised links for clients and vendors.

Deploy and handover

Deployed under your Google account or your server. Handover document covers adding users, adding fields, and backups.
